    What can this IP lookup show?
    
            
    Enter a public IPv4 or IPv6 address to view the network-registration and approximate geolocation data available for that address: country or region, city, postal code, ASN (Autonomous System Number), ISP or organization, coordinates and timezone. Select My IP to inspect the public egress address used by your current connection. IPv6 coverage varies by provider and prefix; some addresses may resolve only to country-level data.
    

            
    Data sources and accuracy
    
            
    When you look up your own IP, the tool uses geolocation data supplied with the request by Cloudflare's edge network; that path does not query third-party IP databases. For a manually entered address, the service requests multiple public IP datasets, including ipwho.is and IPinfo, and returns the first usable response. Requests are proxied through this site for caching and rate limiting. Submitted queries are not persistently stored on our servers; a short-lived technical cache of up to one hour may be used. The result identifies the responding data provider and cache status, with attribution or licensing links where required. IP geolocation reflects provider databases, network registration and routing infrastructure. It is usually approximate at city level and may differ between providers; it is not proof of a device's physical location.

    Frequently asked questions
    
            
    
                Which IP address types are supported?
                
    The lookup accepts public IPv4 and IPv6 addresses, such as 8.8.8.8 or 2001:4860:4860::8888. IPv6 coverage depends on the provider and address range; some results may be limited to country-level information.
    
            
    
            
    
                Are my lookup queries stored?
                
    Requests for manually entered IP addresses may be forwarded to third-party data providers. The site does not persistently store submitted queries on its servers. A short-lived technical cache of up to one hour may be used for performance and rate limiting.
    
            
    
            
    
                Why do IP lookup services return different locations?
                
    Providers maintain different datasets, update schedules and address-range coverage. Their results can differ, especially for mobile, VPN, cloud and recently reassigned networks. Compare the provider shown with each result and treat city-level geolocation as an estimate.
    
            
    
            
    
                Can I inspect raw JSON, client details or lookup history?
                
    Yes. The result area includes a normalized raw JSON view with source and cache details. Client and browser information is read locally in your browser and is not uploaded. Lookup history is stored only in your local browser and can be cleared at any time.
    
            
    
            
    
                What does the CIDR subnet calculator do?
                
    Enter a CIDR range such as 192.168.1.0/24 or a single IP address to calculate the network address, subnet mask, wildcard mask, broadcast address, first and last usable addresses, total address count and usable host count. It supports IPv4 and IPv6, including /31 point-to-point ranges and RFC 5952 IPv6 formatting. Calculations run locally in your browser.
    
            
    
            
    
                Where do DNS and reverse DNS results come from?
                
    DNS queries use Cloudflare's public 1.1.1.1 DNS-over-HTTPS service directly from your browser, not through this site's server. Supported record types include A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS, SOA and CAA. Entering an IP address performs a PTR reverse lookup using the corresponding in-addr.arpa or ip6.arpa name. Non-existent domains are reported as NXDOMAIN.
