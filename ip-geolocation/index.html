What can an IP lookup show?

Depending on the address and the available data providers, a lookup may return country or region, city, postal code, ASN (Autonomous System Number), ISP or organization, coordinates and timezone. IPv4 and IPv6 are supported; coverage and precision vary by provider and address range.

Important: IP geolocation is an estimate based on network registration and provider databases. It does not identify a person or prove a device's physical location. Results are often approximate at city level and may differ between data sources.

How to look up an IP address

  1. Open the IP lookup tool.
  2. Enter a public IPv4 address such as 8.8.8.8, or a public IPv6 address.
  3. Run the lookup and review the available location, ASN, ISP and source details.
  4. Compare the source and timestamp when investigating discrepancies; do not treat an estimate as definitive identity or location evidence.

Privacy and data handling

To return IP geolocation information, the lookup workflow may send the queried IP address to third-party data providers. Review the source and privacy details shown by the tool before submitting sensitive operational information. The CIDR calculation itself runs locally in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can an IP lookup identify a person?

No. An IP address lookup returns network-registration or approximate geolocation data. It does not establish the identity of an individual subscriber or device user.

Does it support IPv6?

Yes. IPv6 lookup coverage depends on the data provider and the address range; some results may be less specific than IPv4 results.

Why do two lookup services show different locations?

Providers maintain different databases, update schedules and coverage. Compare the source information and treat city-level results as estimates.
