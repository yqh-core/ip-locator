Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about IP lookups, location estimates and privacy

Can an IP address reveal someone's exact home address?

No. IP geolocation databases estimate a network's location, often at country, region or city level. They do not provide a reliable street address or identify the person using the connection.

Why does a VPN show a different location?

A VPN routes traffic through an exit server. The lookup usually reports the IP address and approximate location of that exit network, not the device's physical location.

Why can't I look up a private IP such as 192.168.1.1?

Private ranges are reused by millions of local networks and are not globally routed. They do not map to one unique public organization or location.

Does the tool support IPv6?

Yes. IPv4 and IPv6 inputs are supported. Database coverage for IPv6 can be less complete, so some fields may be missing or less precise.

Why do two IP lookup providers show different cities?

Providers maintain separate datasets and update them on different schedules. City-level IP geolocation is an estimate, and a range may be registered to an organization in a different place from the user or server using it.

What does an ASN mean?

An Autonomous System Number identifies a network that participates in internet routing. It helps describe network ownership or routing context, but it does not identify an individual user.

The ASN and organization fields show the registered organization associated with an address range. They do not necessarily show which carrier currently provides access to that address, and they cannot determine whether the user is on a residential, mobile or data center network.

Does IP Locator store the addresses I enter?

The site does not keep a server-side query history. An address you enter is sent to the selected public lookup provider to retrieve its data. Advertising services may process their own data as described in the privacy policy.

Can I use an IP result as proof of identity or location?

No. Treat the result as a troubleshooting clue only. It should not be the sole basis for identity verification, fraud decisions, legal claims or access control.

Is IP Locator free to use?

Yes. The web lookup is free and does not require an account.
